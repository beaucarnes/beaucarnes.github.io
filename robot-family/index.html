Robot Family Uploads

the publishing tool behind the Robot Family channel

Robot Family is an animated series about Zigbo, a small robot, and Barnaby the Iron Golem, set in a Minecraft world. Each episode is built with Blender and Python, and this tool is the last step of that pipeline: it uploads the finished film to the channel and sets its thumbnail through the YouTube Data API.

It is a private tool used only by its developer, Beau Carnes. See the privacy policy for what it accesses and stores.
